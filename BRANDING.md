Apache License 2.0 does not grant trademark rights. The RunTangent name, logo and runtangent.com domain identify the original project; do not present a fork as an official RunTangent service or imply endorsement.

For an independent hosted fork, choose your own product name, logos, artwork, domain, contact and support/payment links. Review template metadata, canonical URLs, sitemap, shared headers, tool titles and `tools.json`; build after changing them. Decorative artwork, raster brand images and favicons are not included. Code, including functional inline SVG icons, is covered by the website code licence; trademark rights remain separate.

No `CNAME` is supplied: a fork must configure its own hosting domain. Existing desktop Store links are external promotional links, not included desktop software.
