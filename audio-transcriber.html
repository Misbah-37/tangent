Local AI Model Required

To transcribe audio offline with total privacy, your browser needs to download a high-precision AI model (~70MB). Browser caching may reduce future downloads. Files are processed locally.

Absolute Privacy Guarantee

By using WebAssembly, the transcription engine runs inside your computer's RAM. Confidential interviews, medical dictations, and private memos never leave your device.

Local Processing

Transcription runs on your computer. Each file is limited to 30 minutes and 100 MB, with an additional decoded-audio memory limit.

Optimized for English

To ensure the AI is lightweight enough to run securely inside your browser without crashing your computer, we utilize a specialized, highly-compressed English-only model.

Frequently Asked Questions
Is my sensitive audio uploaded?

Absolutely not. We consider audio uploads to be a massive security risk. Tangent downloads the transcription "brain" (the AI model) to your computer and processes the audio locally in your browser.

Why does the first run take longer to start?

The AI language model is approximately 70MB. On your very first use, your browser must securely download this file. Your browser may cache model files, but can evict them. Model and library loading may still require an internet connection.

Are there limits on audio length?

Each file can be up to 30 minutes long and 100 MB. A decoded-audio memory limit may require shorter clips, especially for high sample rates or multiple channels. Split longer recordings into smaller files.

What languages are supported?

Currently, this tool supports English only. By restricting the language, the AI model is kept small enough (~70MB) to download quickly and run locally on your hardware. Multilingual models are often too massive for stable browser execution.

How accurate is the offline transcription?

Because recognition runs locally after model loading, it uses a highly compressed "Tiny" AI model. It is incredibly fast and useful for clear voice memos, podcasts, and structured meetings. However, it may occasionally struggle with thick accents, extreme background noise, or heavy overlapping speakers compared to massive, cloud-based commercial APIs.
