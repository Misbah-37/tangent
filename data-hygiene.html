Frequently Asked Questions
What happens to my CSV data when I upload it?

Your data never leaves your device. The Data Sanitizer runs entirely in your browser using a background Web Worker, meaning everything is processed inside your local computer's memory. We do not use external APIs or cloud servers.

Is there a limit to the dataset size I can clean?

The limit is strictly dependent on your device's RAM. Because we utilize Web Workers, the user interface remains responsive, allowing most modern computers to easily process CSV files containing hundreds of thousands of rows.

How does the deduplication process work?

You can select 'Exact Row Match', which only removes a row if every single column is identical to another row. Alternatively, you can select 'Specific Key Columns' to deduplicate based on identifiers like Email Addresses or User IDs.

What is data imputation?

Imputation is the process of replacing missing data with substituted values. Tangent can automatically calculate the Mean (average) or Median of a numeric column and fill in the blank cells for you instantly.
