Is this a digital signature service?

This tool places visual marks on a PDF. It does not verify identity, provide a certificate or create a cryptographic digital signature or audit trail.

How can I create my signature?

Draw a signature, type your name or upload a signature image. Inspect image cleanup before using it, then drag, resize or rotate the mark on your page.

Can I add a date or checkmark?

Yes. Add a date or checkmark to the current page, then position it on the document. Use the page controls to move between pages.

What happens to PDF form fields?

Leave flattening unchecked to preserve supported editable forms; stamps must not overlap their fields. Flattening keeps visible values but removes editable fields. Review the exported copy and keep the original.

Where are my documents processed?

Selected documents and signature images are processed in browser memory. PDF libraries load from external CDNs, so network access may be needed. Download your result before closing the page; browser memory limits apply.
