100% Client-Side Privacy Guarantee

Your Files Stay on Your Device. Period.

Tangent executes entirely within your browser's local sandbox memory. We never transmit your files, log telemetry, or require accounts. (The sole exception is our Password Shield, which securely queries an anonymous partial hash against the HaveIBeenPwned database).

Zero Server Footprint

Your files, screenshots, audio, and credentials never touch remote servers. In-memory execution guarantees total data confidentiality.

No Accounts, Ads, or Watermarks

Every tool is 100% free forever. No credit cards, no paywalls, no email sign-ups, and zero branded watermarks stamped on your exports.

Offline & Hardware Accelerated

Once loaded, the entire toolkit operates completely offline. Heavy computations leverage your device's native CPU and GPU with zero network lag.

Frequently Asked Questions
How does Tangent guarantee 100% client-side privacy?

Every single utility in Tangent is built using modern WebAssembly, HTML5 Canvas, and Web Crypto APIs that run directly inside your web browser sandbox. Your files are processed entirely in your computer's RAM and never transmitted across the network.

Are there any hidden costs, usage limits, or subscriptions?

No. Tangent is 100% free and open. There are no 5-minute video recording limits, no page count caps on PDFs, no watermarks, and no sign-up forms.

Can I use Tangent tools when I am disconnected from the internet?

Yes. Once you load a tool page, all required WebAssembly modules and scripts are cached by your browser. You can turn off your Wi-Fi or go into airplane mode and continue using the tools with zero interruptions.
