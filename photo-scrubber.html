    Client-Side Photo Privacy Architecture

    Whenever you take a photograph using a modern smartphone or digital camera, the device automatically stamps invisible metadata—known as EXIF (Exchangeable Image File Format)—directly into the file structure. While harmless in a personal album, sharing unscrubbed photos online can compromise your physical security, family privacy, and personal data.

    Why Photo Metadata Can Be Dangerous

    • GPS Home Geolocation: Modern smartphones record the exact geographic coordinates (latitude, longitude, and elevation) where an image was captured. A photo taken of your pet or living room can pinpoint your exact house and apartment number to anyone who inspects the file.
    • Device & Hardware Fingerprinting: EXIF tags embed the camera manufacturer, device model, software build, and unique camera serial numbers. Stalkers and data scrapers use these identifiers to cross-reference photos across multiple online profiles.
    • Timestamps & Routines: Photos reveal the exact date, hour, minute, and second a picture was snapped, inadvertently advertising your family's daily commute, vacation periods, and routine habits.

    How Our In-Browser Scrubber Works

    Unlike conventional photo editors that require you to upload your personal photos to a remote cloud server, our Photo Scrubber operates entirely inside your web browser's memory using HTML5 Canvas rendering. When you clean a photo, the browser extracts the raw visual pixels onto an isolated local drawing canvas and re-encodes a fresh file, completely discarding all EXIF tags, GPS blocks, IPTC captions, and embedded thumbnails. Zero bytes leave your device.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is EXIF metadata and why is it dangerous?

    EXIF metadata is hidden technical information recorded automatically by your smartphone or camera every time you snap a picture. It frequently includes your exact GPS coordinates (latitude and longitude accurate to your home address), camera model, and timestamp.

    Do social media sites and marketplaces remove EXIF data?

    While some platforms like Instagram strip metadata upon upload, many classified sites, forums, chat apps, and email clients preserve full original EXIF tags, exposing your private location to anyone who downloads the image.

    Does this tool upload my photos to an external server?

    No. The Photo EXIF & GPS Scrubber operates 100% inside your web browser using HTML5 Canvas rasterization and client-side JavaScript. Zero bytes are ever transmitted over the network.

    Does scrubbing metadata degrade photo quality?

    No noticeable loss occurs. The scrubber re-encodes pure image pixels at 95% quality (or your custom selected quality) while permanently omitting all hidden binary metadata blocks.
