How to Create Reliable Custom QR Codes

QR codes (Quick Response codes) provide a frictionless bridge between physical environments and digital destinations. Tangent's offline generator encodes your data directly into the matrix with zero intermediate tracking servers, preventing link decay or third-party redirection downtime.

Best Practices for High Scan Reliability

  • Ensure Sufficient Contrast: Always keep the foreground modules substantially darker than the background canvas. Classic black on white or dark navy on cream delivers the highest optical readability across all lighting conditions.
  • High Error Correction Level ('H'): Tangent defaults to Level H error correction (30% redundancy), ensuring your QR code remains decodable even if partially smudged, creased, or printed on textured materials.
  • Wi-Fi Network QR Codes: When generating a guest Wi-Fi code, mobile cameras automatically parse the SSID and WPA key, letting visitors connect in a single tap without manual password entry.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do these QR codes expire?

No, they never expire. The QR codes generated here are static, meaning your text, URL, or Wi-Fi credentials are mathematically embedded directly into the matrix pattern. They work permanently without reliance on third-party redirection servers.

Is my data stored or tracked?

No. The QR Code Generator executes 100% client-side in your web browser using JavaScript. No URLs, Wi-Fi passwords, or contact info are ever sent across the network.

How does the Wi-Fi QR code work?

It uses the universal standard WIFI: syntax recognized natively by iOS and Android cameras. When guests scan the code, their phone prompts them to join the Wi-Fi network instantly without typing the password.
