Frequently Asked Questions
Do these QR codes expire?

No, they never expire. The QR codes generated here are static, meaning your text, URL, or Wi-Fi credentials are mathematically embedded directly into the matrix pattern. They work permanently without reliance on third-party redirection servers.

Can the QR codes be scanned when printed on physical materials?

Yes. With standard error correction (up to 30%), the generated QR codes scan reliably on business cards, menus, packaging, and outdoor banners.

Is my Wi-Fi password or personal info stored when generating?

No. All QR matrix computation happens in your browser using local JavaScript. Your Wi-Fi network keys and contacts are never transmitted or logged.
