Frequently Asked Questions

Is there any recording time limit or video cap?

No. Commercial SaaS tools like Loom or Screencastify aggressively cut free recordings off after 5 minutes and demand a monthly subscription. Tangent has zero arbitrary caps—you can record full meetings, tutorials, coding walkthroughs, or software bug reports for as long as your computer has RAM.

Can I record both microphone voiceover and system sound?

Yes! Tangent uses the Web Audio API to mix your microphone input and internal system/tab audio into a unified stereo audio stream. When prompted by your browser to share your screen, make sure to check "Share audio" (or "Share tab audio") to include internal computer sounds.

Does Tangent place any watermarks on the exported video?

Never. Every recording exported from Tangent is 100% clean, crisp, and watermark-free.

Are my recordings sent to the cloud?

No. Your video streams are encoded directly by your web browser and saved into RAM memory. Zero bytes of video data are ever transmitted across the network.
