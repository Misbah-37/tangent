Frequently Asked Questions
Is there any recording time limit or video cap?

No. Commercial SaaS tools aggressively cut free recordings off after 5 minutes and demand a monthly subscription. Tangent has zero arbitrary caps—you can record full meetings, tutorials, coding walkthroughs, or software bug reports for as long as your computer has available memory.

Can I record system audio along with microphone sound?

Yes. When selecting a browser tab or entire screen, check the 'Share audio' option to capture high-fidelity desktop sound and YouTube/video audio alongside your microphone narration.

Where are my recorded video files stored?

Recordings are saved directly to your device's Downloads directory upon stopping. The tool never uploads or caches video bytes on remote servers.
