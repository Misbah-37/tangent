Frequently Asked Questions
Is my proprietary code uploaded to any third-party server?

No. Code-to-Image Studio operates 100% locally inside your browser memory using HTML5 Canvas and client-side rendering. Your code, proprietary algorithms, and tokens are never uploaded or tracked.

How do I export high-resolution (Retina) images?

The export engine natively renders the snippet at 2x Retina resolution onto an HTML5 Canvas, ensuring crisp typography and razor-sharp gradients for slides, documentation, and social media.

Can I copy the generated screenshot directly to my clipboard?

Yes. Simply click 'Copy Image to Clipboard' to write the high-resolution PNG directly into your operating system clipboard for instant pasting into Slack, Notion, Discord, or Figma.
