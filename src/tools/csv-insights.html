Frequently Asked Questions
Is my CSV data uploaded to a server?

No. The CSV Insights Studio runs completely inside your web browser using HTML5 and client-side JavaScript. Your data never leaves your computer. We don't even have a database to store it in.

How does the Auto-Profiler work?

When you upload a file, a background Web Worker scans every column to detect data types, calculate averages, and map distributions, generating instant mini-charts without locking up your browser.

Can I save the charts I build?

Yes! Once you build a chart in the Manual Explorer or view the Auto-Profiler, you can click 'Save Chart as Image' to download a high-resolution PNG locally for your presentations.
