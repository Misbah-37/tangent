Frequently Asked Questions
Is my CSV data uploaded to a server?

No. This tool runs completely inside your web browser using HTML5 and vanilla client-side JavaScript. Your data never leaves your computer, and there's no third-party library loaded from a CDN either.

How does the Auto-Profiler work?

When you upload a file, a background Web Worker scans every column to detect its type (numeric, date, or categorical), computes statistics like median and standard deviation, and builds a histogram — all without freezing your browser.

Can I save the charts I build?

Yes — every chart, in both the Auto-Profiler and the Manual Explorer, has a "Save as PNG" option that renders straight from the canvas, no server round-trip needed.
