Frequently Asked Questions
Are my screenshots uploaded to your servers?

Never. Tangent tools operate 100% client-side inside your browser memory using HTML5 Canvas. Your image pixels never traverse the network or touch any backend server.

How does clipboard paste work?

Simply take a screenshot on your computer using Windows Snipping Tool (Win + Shift + S) or macOS Screenshot (Cmd + Shift + 4), then press Ctrl + V (or Cmd + V) anywhere inside this tab. The image will load automatically.

What export formats and resolutions are supported?

You can export in PNG or JPEG at 1x or 2x high-resolution Retina pixel densities, ideal for crisp presentation on 4K monitors and mobile retina displays.
