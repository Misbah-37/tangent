      Frequently Asked Questions
      Is it safe to upload secure forms and bank statements here?

      You aren't actually 'uploading' anything. All the code that powers this PDF tool is downloaded to your browser the moment you visit the page. When you select a file, processing happens directly in your computer's memory. No data is transmitted over the internet.

      Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

      Encrypted PDFs require decryption before assembly. If your PDF has an open password, unlock it before merging. Permissions-locked documents can be processed directly.

      What happens to my PDF files when I close the tab?

      All memory buffers allocated for your PDF are freed immediately by the browser's garbage collector. Zero files are retained on disk or cached on remote servers.
