Frequently Asked Questions
Are my receipts uploaded to a server?

No. We utilize an Apache 2.0 licensed WebAssembly OCR engine that processes your financial documents entirely in your local RAM. No servers or APIs are involved.

Can it read handwritten tips on restaurant receipts?

The Tesseract OCR engine is highly optimized for printed typography (like thermal receipt printers). It may struggle to accurately identify messy handwritten tip amounts and signatures.
