Local OCR Engine Required

To scan receipts without uploading them to the cloud, your browser needs to download the Tesseract Optical Character Recognition engine (~25MB). Browser caching may reduce later downloads. An internet connection may still be needed.

Confidential Expense Tracking

Financial documents contain highly sensitive merchant data and personal locations. By running OCR on your local machine, zero data leaves your network.

Smart Regex Parsing

After extracting the raw text, the tool utilizes advanced Regular Expressions to automatically locate and isolate the most likely purchase dates and total amounts.

Apache 2.0 Tesseract Engine

Powered by Tesseract.js, a WebAssembly port of the industry-standard OCR engine. Review recognition results, especially for small type and damaged receipts.

Frequently Asked Questions
Are my receipts uploaded to a server?

No. We utilize an Apache 2.0 licensed WebAssembly OCR engine that processes your financial documents entirely in your local RAM. Engine and language files may need downloading; receipt content is processed locally.

Can it read handwritten tips on restaurant receipts?

The Tesseract OCR engine is highly optimized for printed typography (like thermal receipt printers). It may struggle to accurately identify messy handwritten tip amounts and signatures.
