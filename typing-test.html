How to Improve Your Typing Speed

Whether you are coding, writing emails, or drafting essays, typing speed is a critical productivity multiplier. Our free typing speed test is designed to help you benchmark your current Words Per Minute (WPM) and track your progress over time.

Top Tips for Faster Typing

  • Master the Home Row: Rest your fingers on the ASDF and JKL; keys. This minimizes finger travel distance and builds muscle memory.
  • Accuracy Over Speed: Do not rush. Hitting backspace destroys your WPM. Focus on hitting the correct keys slowly, and speed will naturally follow.
  • Look at the Screen, Not the Keyboard: Touch typing requires trusting your hands. If you look down, you break your flow and lose your place in the text.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good typing speed?

The average typing speed is around 40 WPM. A speed of 60 WPM is considered good and is often required for professional administrative jobs. Programmers and writers frequently average between 70 and 90 WPM.

How is WPM calculated?

Words Per Minute (WPM) is standardized based on characters typed, not literal words. One "word" is calculated as 5 keystrokes. We take your total keystrokes, divide by 5, and adjust for the time elapsed.
