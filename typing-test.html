Frequently Asked Questions
What is a good typing speed?

The global average typing speed is roughly 40 WPM. A speed of 60 WPM is considered solid and often required for administrative or legal transcription jobs. Experienced software engineers and professional typists frequently average between 70 and 95 WPM.

How is WPM (Words Per Minute) calculated?

In standard typing metrics, one 'word' is defined as exactly 5 keystrokes (including spaces). Gross WPM is total typed words divided by minutes elapsed. Net WPM subtracts uncorrected errors.

Why is accuracy more important than raw speed?

Typing with 98%+ accuracy creates steady momentum. Correcting typos wastes significant time and breaks muscle memory rhythm.
